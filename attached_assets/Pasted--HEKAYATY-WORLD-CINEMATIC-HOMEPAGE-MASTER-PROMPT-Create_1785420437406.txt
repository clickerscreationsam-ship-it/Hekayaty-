# HEKAYATY WORLD — CINEMATIC HOMEPAGE MASTER PROMPT

Create an ultra-premium AAA-quality homepage for a fictional entertainment universe called **Hekayaty World**.

The homepage must feel like a combination of:

* League of Legends Universe
* Arcane Official Website
* Riot Forge
* Marvel Unlimited
* DC Universe Infinite
* Star Wars Databank

But with a completely original identity.

The result should look like a website that cost more than **$50,000** to design and build.

The design must immediately communicate:

* Epic storytelling
* Massive universe
* Legendary heroes
* Ancient civilizations
* Fantasy worlds
* Futuristic technology
* Premium entertainment brand

The website is NOT a comic store.

It is the official digital hub of a massive Arabic fantasy and superhero universe.
